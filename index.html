YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy

This storefront demo stores your cart, wishlist, order history, product catalog and contact messages in local storage in the browser you are using. The demo does not send those records to a shared database, so they are not available on other devices and may be removed when browser data is cleared.

When you choose to order, the site opens WhatsApp with your order details so you can send them to Azza Organics. WhatsApp handles that message under its own privacy terms. Please do not enter sensitive personal information into this demo.

For questions about information you have sent to us, contact azaaorganics@gmail.com.

BEFORE YOU ORDER

Terms & conditions

Placing an order through this demo sends an order request only. There is no online payment. Azza Organics will confirm product availability, final pricing and delivery arrangements with you on WhatsApp before the order is accepted.

Product photos and descriptions are provided to help you browse. Product sizes, prices and availability may change; the details confirmed by Azza Organics at the time of your order will apply.

By submitting an order request, you confirm that the contact and delivery details you provide are accurate and that Azza Organics may use them to follow up about that request.
